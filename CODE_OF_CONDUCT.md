# Institutional Code of Conduct

## Medical Student Information System (MSIS)
### Southwestern University PHINMA • School of Medicine

---

## 🏛️ Our Pledge

The **Medical Student Information System (MSIS)** at **Southwestern University PHINMA School of Medicine** is dedicated to fostering a secure, respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for all community members—including medical students, faculty members, clinicians, department secretaries, administrative staff, and IT personnel.

We pledge that everyone who participates in, contributes to, or utilizes the MSIS Support Ticket System and its associated repositories will experience an environment free from discrimination, harassment, and unauthorized disclosure of confidential academic or clinical data.

---

## 🎯 Expected Behaviors

All users, contributors, and maintainers of the MSIS platform are expected to:

- **Exercise Professional Empathy:** Treat fellow students, faculty, triage officers, and technical engineers with dignity, courtesy, and respect.
- **Protect Confidentiality:** Ensure all submitted tickets, screenshots, and diagnostic logs strictly redact any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive health/patient data in compliance with the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**.
- **Focus on Constructive Collaboration:** Provide clear, actionable technical details when reporting issues or requesting feature enhancements.
- **Respect Operational Workflows:** Follow designated role boundaries and priority designations without misrepresenting urgent emergencies or tampering with triage queues.
- **Uphold Academic & Medical Integrity:** Adhere to the ethical standards established by the Southwestern University PHINMA School of Medicine.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behaviors

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited across the MSIS Support platform, ticket correspondence, and source code collaboration:

1. **Abusive or Derogatory Language:** Profanity, insults, personal attacks, or disparaging remarks directed at students, faculty, secretaries, or technical support personnel.
2. **Harassment & Discrimination:** Offensive comments, intimidation, or exclusion based on race, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, age, religion, or academic standing.
3. **Unauthorized Data Exposure:** Uploading or disseminating unredacted medical records, patient identifiers, student exam answers, academic transcripts, or unencrypted system credentials.
4. **Platform Abuse & Vandalism:** Submitting fraudulent or duplicate tickets to artificially skew triage queues, spamming endpoints, or exploiting security vulnerabilities without responsible disclosure.
5. **Impersonation:** Posing as another student, faculty member, department head, or systems administrator.

---

## 🔒 Data Privacy & Medical Information Safeguards

Given the clinical and educational nature of the Southwestern University PHINMA School of Medicine:

- **Zero Tolerance for Patient Health Information (PHI) in Tickets:** Tickets and file attachments must never contain identifiable patient information, clinical case study records, or hospital charts.
- **System Credential Security:** Passwords, API tokens, session cookies, and private database credentials must never be posted in public issues, pull requests, or ticket descriptions.
- **Compliance:** All operations must comply with institutional data governance policies and the National Privacy Commission (NPC) regulations of the Philippines.

---

## 🛡️ Enforcement & Reporting

### Reporting an Incident
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, harassment, or data privacy breaches within the MSIS Support platform or development repository:

- **Contact MSIS Administration:** Email the engineering team at `support@swu-som.com` or `medsis798@gmail.com`.
- **School of Medicine Oversight:** For severe behavioral violations, reports may be escalated directly to the Office of the Dean, School of Medicine, Southwestern University PHINMA.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated with the highest degree of confidentiality and fairness.

### Enforcement Actions
Maintainers and administrators have the authority to implement the following corrective measures:

1. **Correction:** A private written clarification and request to amend inappropriate behavior or redact sensitive content.
2. **Formal Warning:** An official written warning detailing the violation and consequences of continued misconduct.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** Revocation of access to the support system, repositories, or triage queues for a designated period.
4. **Permanent Ban & Institutional Referral:** Permanent termination of access and escalation to the Southwestern University PHINMA Disciplinary Committee or Human Resources Department.
